Week 1: Introduction to Arabic Language & Script

The Alphabet (Al-Abjadiyah): Recognizing, naming, and writing all 28 letters.

Letter Positions: Initial, Medial, and Final forms of each letter.

Short Vowels (Harakat): Fatha (a), Kasra (i), Damma (u), and Sukun (no vowel).

Long Vowels (Mad): Alif, Waw, and Ya.

Basic Phonetics: Mastering difficult sounds (e.g., 'Ain, Ghayn, Qaf, and the emphatic letters).

Greetings 101: Common phrases (Assalamu Alaikum, Marhaba, Sabah al-Khair).

Week 2: Basic Vocabulary & Grammar (The Essentials)

Vocabulary: Numbers: Cardinal numbers 1–100 and ordinal numbers (1st, 2nd, etc.).

Vocabulary: Colors: Primary and secondary colors, and their masculine vs. feminine forms.

Vocabulary: Common Nouns: Everyday objects (book, pen, chair, door, house).

Grammar: Definite vs. Indefinite: Using "Al-" (The) and Tanween (a/an).

Grammar: Nouns & Adjectives: The rule of agreement (Gender, Number, and Definiteness).

Sun and Moon Letters: Identifying which letters "absorb" the 'L' in "Al-".

Week 3: Verbs & Sentence Structure

The Root System: Understanding how 3-letter roots (F-'-L) create families of words.

Basic Verb Conjugation: Introducing the "Madi" (Past) tense for singular pronouns.

Personal Pronouns: I, You (m/f), He, She, We, They.

Nominal Sentences (Jumla Ismiya): Creating simple "Subject + Predicate" sentences (e.g., "The book is new").

Verbal Sentences (Jumla Fi'liya): Understanding the "Verb + Subject + Object" order.

Week 4: Practical Literacy

Spelling Rules: Joining letters and identifying "non-connector" letters (Alif, Dal, Thal, Ra, Zay, Waw).

Special Marks: Shadda (doubled consonant) and Hamza positions.

Reading Fluency: Decoding simple sentences and short paragraphs.

Writing Practice: Dictation exercises and basic transcription.
